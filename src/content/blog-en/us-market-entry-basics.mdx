## Introduction

Many Japanese food manufacturers share the same wish: "We want to sell in America." But once they actually try to enter the market, they run into all kinds of barriers: the language gap, different business customs, and a complex distribution network.

That is why **local sales representation services** are drawing attention.

## The US Food Market Today

The US food retail market is the largest in the world, with annual sales in the trillions of dollars. The Japanese food boom in particular has continued in recent years, and interest in Japanese food brands such as Sushi, Ramen, and Matcha keeps growing year after year.

To seize this big opportunity, however, a partner who knows the local distribution structure inside and out is essential.

## Three Challenges a Sales Representation Service Solves

### 1. Language and Cultural Barriers
A local team handles all of your business communication, including presentations in English, contract negotiations, and follow-ups.

### 2. Building a Distribution Network

### 3. Ongoing Shelf Management
After your product is placed, we continue to support you with inventory management, shelf-space negotiations, and promotional follow-ups, building a system that keeps your product selling.
